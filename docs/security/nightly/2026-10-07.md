### 1. Legacy webhook_queue delivery bypassed the URL policy (fixed)
- Root cause: when the job queue is unavailable or its module is off, `rivetEmitEvent()` writes to `webhook_queue` and `cron/cron.php` delivered with `file_get_contents()` on the stored URL. That sends no policy re-check at call time, re-resolves DNS (rebinding), and the http stream wrapper follows redirects, so a URL that passed the save-time check could redirect to loopback, link-local or cloud-metadata targets. It also read the URL without `decryptSetting()`, although platform webhook URLs are now stored encrypted, so those deliveries could never succeed.
- Impact: blind SSRF (POST of event JSON) from the server, outside the admin's allowed-networks policy. Requires an admin-created webhook or control of a DNS answer or redirect on an allowed public URL.
- Fix: decrypt the URL, vet it with `rivetWebhookResolveTarget()`, and deliver through `WebhookDispatcher::curlOptions()` and `pinnedUrl()` (pinned addresses, no redirects, TLS verified, http/https only), the same as the `send_webhook` rule action. Unsafe URLs are logged and follow the existing retry/failed path.
- Test: tests/webhook_queue_delivery.php.

### 2. Portal technical contact could take over a billing or technical contact (fixed)
- Root cause: a non-primary technical contact may edit other contacts. Role fields were already protected, but the contact's email and auth method (the `users` row that drives login and password reset) could be changed, so the editor could re-point a billing contact's login to their own mailbox, reset the password and inherit billing rights (charging the saved card via `add_payment_by_provider`).
- Impact: privilege escalation inside one client tenant (technical to billing/technical).
- Fix: a non-primary editor is refused for any contact with technical or billing rights unless it is their own record. The primary contact is unaffected.
- Test: tests/webhook_queue_delivery.php (source-order assertions; the handler needs a database).
